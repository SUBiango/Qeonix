Sovereign by design

Deployment is an architectural decision,not a hosting invoice.

For government and regulated operators, where the system runs and who can reach the data is part of the design brief. We treat it that way from the first architecture session.

  • Public cloud

    Where speed matters more than isolation and the data class allows it.

    01

  • Private cloud

    Dedicated tenancy under the organization's own controls.

    02

  • On-premise

    Inside the customer's own data center and network boundary.

    03

  • Isolated / sovereign

    Architected for data residency and disconnected environments.

    04

  • Identity and role-based access

    Every agent, service and operator acts under an identity, with permissions that can be reviewed and revoked.

  • Auditability

    Actions, inputs and model decisions are recorded so a reviewer can reconstruct what happened and why.

  • Human oversight

    Consequential actions stop at a defined checkpoint. Autonomy is granted deliberately, per workflow.

  • Controlled model access

    Open-source or commercial models, routed per workload, with the option to keep inference inside your boundary.

  • Data residency

    Deployment topologies designed to keep data in the jurisdiction the mandate requires.

  • Security engineering

    Threat modeling, secrets handling and hardening treated as engineering work, not a final checklist.
