The chain

Sense, understand, decide, act, learn.

Physical AI is not a robot. It is a closed loop, and the value is in closing it: most deployments stop at capture and never reach a work order.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Cameras, lidar, thermal, telemetry and field reports on one clock.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Vision models classify condition, defect, obstruction and change.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Severity, risk threshold and standing authority determine the response.

  4. 04

    Act

    A mission launches, a crew is tasked, an asset is taken out of service.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Confirmed outcomes retrain the model and adjust the threshold.

Safety and assurance

Autonomy under authority.

  • Supervised by default

    Autonomy handles the routine pass. A qualified operator holds authority over anything outside the cleared envelope.

  • Fail-safe behavior

    Loss of link, low power and sensor failure have defined, tested responses, not emergent ones.

  • Evidence, not assertions

    Every mission produces a retained, timestamped, geo-referenced record that can be reviewed after the fact.

  • Operating within the rules

    Missions are designed around the airspace, site and permitting regime that applies, established with the operator before flight.

Questions

Autonomous systems, answered.

We select and integrate proven platforms rather than manufacture airframes. What Qeonix builds is the layer that makes a fleet of them useful: mission planning, perception, the operations console, the evidence trail and the integration into asset and maintenance systems.

Autonomy is granted per mission type, not as a general property. A repeated inspection pass over a known site with a cleared route is a good candidate. Anything novel, congested or safety-critical stays under direct supervision with defined fail-safe behavior.

Operating approvals are held by the operating party and are established for each site and mission profile with the relevant authority. We design mission profiles and record-keeping to fit that regime, and we do not fly outside it.
