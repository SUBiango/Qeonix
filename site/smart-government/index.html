The journey

Seven steps between a needand a closed case.

In most entities, four of these steps are where the time goes, and none of them are the decision itself.

  1. 01

    A resident has a need

    Expressed in their own words, on the channel they already use, in Arabic or English.

  2. 02

    One front door

    A unified digital service surface instead of a directory of departments.

  3. 03

    Identity and eligibility

    Verified once against digital identity, then checked against the record rather than re-asked.

  4. 04

    Orchestration across agencies

    The case is assembled and routed across every department it actually touches.

  5. 05

    Decision and action

    Automated where policy allows, escalated where it does not, with the reason recorded.

  6. 06

    Payment, permit, dispatch

    The transaction completes, or a crew is tasked in the physical world.

  7. 07

    Closed and evidenced

    Notification to the resident, an audit trail for the auditor, a metric for the mandate.

Outcomes

What changes for the entity.

  • Fewer steps for the resident

    Documents already held by government are not requested again. Eligibility is checked against the record, not the applicant's memory.

  • Fewer hand-offs between agencies

    A case moves as one object with one history, instead of restarting each time it crosses a departmental boundary.

  • Visible operational truth

    Backlog, cycle time and field capacity are visible while they can still be changed, not in next quarter's report.

  • Defensible decisions

    Every automated or assisted decision carries the rule applied, the data used and the officer who approved it.

  • Capacity where it is scarce

    Routine volume is absorbed by the platform so scarce specialist time goes to the cases that need judgment.

  • Sovereignty preserved

    Resident data stays inside the deployment boundary the mandate requires, including in disconnected environments.
